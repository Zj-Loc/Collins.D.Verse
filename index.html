    About

    I am an innovative IT specialist, web developer, and creative designer with a passion for technology and problem-solving. My journey in IT has been fueled by curiosity and a drive to create solutions that make life easier, smarter, and more engaging. I specialize in building modern, responsive websites, delivering IT support and troubleshooting, and designing visual content that connects with people.
    Over the years, I’ve worked on diverse projects — from developing tech platforms like Tech Hub and Parcel-App, to designing event posters and branding materials for DJ Collombo. This mix of technical and creative work has sharpened my ability to merge design thinking with technical execution, ensuring that every project I touch is both functional and visually striking.
    I am proficient in Microsoft Office Suite (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Access), which supports my ability to deliver professional documentation, data analysis, and impactful presentations. Alongside this, I bring expertise in web technologies (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Node.js), graphic design tools, and IT troubleshooting. My approach is not just about solving problems, but also about crafting experiences that are reliable, efficient, and inspiring.
    What sets me apart is my adaptability and creativity — I thrive whether I’m coding a web application, designing an eye-catching poster, analyzing data in Excel, or guiding someone through IT support. I enjoy collaborating, but I’m equally confident working independently to see projects through from concept to completion. Driven by a vision to become a well-rounded IT expert, I’m always learning, experimenting, and exploring new ways to blend technology with creativity. My ultimate goal is to provide solutions that add value, whether for individuals, businesses, or entire communities.
